| 11 | 11 Oct 2026 | Entity schemas are derived from each project's **train** split only, not from all its data. | A developer declares a schema without having seen the evaluation cases. | 13 | 11 Oct 2026 | Out-of-scope cases are sampled disjointly across the three projects. | The plan forbids drawing out-of-scope cases from another project's domains; disjoint sampling additionally stops one utterance serving as evidence for two projects. |
| 14 | 11 Oct 2026 | The catalogue's TF-IDF out-of-scope policy rejects a request **before** any model call, so a rejected case costs zero calls. | It is the honest reading of a similarity-threshold policy, and it makes the efficiency difference between the two out-of-scope policies measurable. Threshold rejections are excluded from latency figures and flagged in `timing.jsonl`, so they cannot flatter a configuration's latency. |
| 15 | 11 Oct 2026 | Two threshold-tuning rules, both development-only: B0 and B3 maximise macro-F1 over intents plus the out-of-scope class; the catalogue's TF-IDF policy maximises F1 on the binary in-scope/out-of-scope decision. | The catalogue policy decides before the model call, so no intent prediction exists at tuning time and the macro-F1 rule cannot be applied. Both rules break ties toward the lower threshold, which rejects fewer in-scope requests. |
| 16 | 11 Oct 2026 | Intent and entity *descriptions* are absent from the bare `ProjectSpec` and are added only by the Analyzer (G6) or by hand for B2. Names come from the manifest and the Analyzer may never change them. | Authoritative supplied fields take precedence over inferred ones (plan section 6), and it makes the no-Analyzer ablation a real comparison: the ablation runs on the bare specification, A1 on the enriched one. |
| 17 | 11 Oct 2026 | B0 is trained on the same k = 10 per intent pool as every LLM system, and extracts no entities. | Matches the data regime so the comparison is fair; B0 is an intent classifier, so its entity F1 is 0 by design rather than by failure. Reported plainly. |
| 18 | 11 Oct 2026 | The B3 Kaggle notebook **exports predictions and computes no metrics**. Scoring happens on the laptop with the shared scorer and span rule. | Metrics computed in the notebook by different code would not be comparable with every other system's. This keeps one scorer for all systems. |
| 19 | 11 Oct 2026 | The file uploaded to Kaggle (`data/manifests/b3_splits.json`) contains **only the k-shot pools and development splits for P1 and P2** - no test labels, nothing from P3. | Enforces the freeze and the held-out-project rule by construction rather than by remembering: the notebook cannot touch them because they are not present. Also avoids sending test labels to a third-party service. Guarded by `tests/test_b3_assets.py`. |
| 20 | 11 Oct 2026 | B3's entity output is BIO-decoded to the utterance's own substrings and then located by the shared span rule, rather than passing its exact offsets straight through. | Plan section 5 requires one evaluator for every system. The strings are true substrings, so they always locate; the only cost is that a repeated token may be matched at a different occurrence, which is recorded as a limitation. |
| 21 | 11 Oct 2026 | The candidate selection rule lives in one module (`src/evaluation/selection.py`) used by **both** B4 and the agent. | B4 exists to test whether the agent's *choice of candidates* helps. If B4 and A1 ranked candidates by different logic, any difference between them would be uninterpretable - it could be the search or it could be the ranking. |
| 22 | 11 Oct 2026 | A declared p95 latency constraint is filtered on a **declared estimate** at selection time, and the selected pipeline's real p95 is then measured in the uncached timing run. Where no estimate exists the constraint is recorded as `unchecked`, never silently passed. | Cached evaluation runs carry no latency by design, and timing all 12 candidates per project would cost far more calls than the budget allows. Recording "unchecked" keeps the gap visible instead of implying the constraint was met. |
| 23 | 11 Oct 2026 | B4-random is reported as a **distribution over 5 seeds**, and seeds where no candidate was eligible are counted rather than dropped. | A single random draw is noise; comparing A1 against one seed would be comparing against noise. Dropping failed seeds would bias the baseline upward. |
| 24 | 11 Oct 2026 | Local model weights are obtained as a GGUF over a resumable HTTP range download and registered with `data/models/Modelfile.qwen3b`, rather than by `ollama pull`. | `ollama pull` failed three times on a slow connection because it restarts chunks and gives up; `curl -C -` resumes at the exact byte. Same model family and quantization. The weights are gitignored; the Modelfile is tracked so the setup stays reproducible. |
